# Zeva Platform: Legal Documents, Certifications & Commercial Launch Audit

This document catalogs the essential **Legal Documents, Regulatory Certifications, Payment Gateway KYC Requirements, AI Compliance Disclosures, and Codebase Enhancements** mandatory for legally launching the Zeva AI Chatbot SaaS platform into live global and Indian commercial production.

---

## 1. Public Website Legal Policies (Mandatory for Commercial Operations)
To legally operate a paid SaaS application and protect against civil liabilities, the public website must display accessible, legally binding policy pages. Currently, the application contains **zero dedicated legal route pages** in `fortend/src/app`.

### Core Policy Checklist
- [x] **Terms of Service (ToS) / Terms of Use (`/terms`)**:
  * **Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)**: Strictly prohibits utilizing Zeva to build chatbots for malicious automated spam, abusive RAG indexing, scraping without permission, spreading misinformation, or distributing illegal content.
  * **Limitation of Liability & AI Disclaimers**: Explicitly disclaims legal responsibility for generative AI inaccuracies, hallucinations, or automated advice generated by tenant bots in medical, real estate, financial, or legal domains.
  * **Termination of Service**: Reserves the right for platform administrators to immediately suspend or terminate accounts engaging in Terms of Service violations or payment defaults without reimbursement.
- [x] **Privacy Policy (`/privacy`)**:
  * Details personal data collection classes: authentication identities (Better Auth JWTs), billing metadata, uploaded company documents (`/ingest-file`), conversation transcripts (`chats`), and lead contact data (`leads`).
  * Discloses compliance jurisdictional protections under **GDPR** (EU Article 13 & 14), **CCPA/CPRA** (California Consumer Privacy Act), and **DPDPA 2023** (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, India).
  * Lists data retention duration models and user procedures for executing Subject Access & Erasure Requests.
- [x] **Refund & Subscription Cancellation Policy (`/refund-policy`)**:
  * **Critical Payment Gateway Prerequisite**: Both Stripe and Razorpay **strictly require** an active, publicly accessible refund and recurring billing cancellation policy page linked on checkout screens before approving merchant accounts for live transaction processing.
  * Defines Pro/Enterprise billing cycle cancellation rules, prorated credit guidelines, and explicit refund dispute submission windows (e.g., 7-day or 14-day refund eligibility terms).
- [x] **Cookie & Telemetry Disclosure Policy (`/cookies`)**:
  * Explains the deployment of functional authentication session cookies, Stripe/Razorpay fraud prevention scripts, and anonymous UI telemetry monitoring.

## 2. Payment Gateway Regulatory KYC & Certifications
Operating dual checkout systems (Stripe for USD/Global and Razorpay for INR/India) mandates formal corporate KYC filings and compliance with national banking frameworks.

### Razorpay Live Business KYC (India Operations)
- [x] **Corporate Registration Verification**: Certificate of Incorporation (Private Limited Company, LLP, or registered Sole Proprietorship).
- [x] **Tax & Identity Credentials**: Corporate PAN (Permanent Account Number) Card and active GSTIN (Goods & Services Tax Identification Number) registration certificate.
- [x] **Corporate Bank Linking**: Verified commercial corporate banking statement or cancelled cheque matching the exact business registration entity.
- [x] **Reserve Bank of India (RBI) e-Mandate Framework Compliance**: To lawfully charge recurring Indian credit/debit cards or UPI subscriptions automatically, the checkout architecture must adhere to RBI guidelines requiring mandatory pre-debit notifications (SMS/Email alerts issued 24 hours prior to recurring auto-debits).

### Stripe Live Global Verification (Global Operations)
- [x] **Entity Identity Authentication**: Legal entity identifier (US EIN, UK CRN, or global corporate equivalents) alongside verified government identification for beneficial ownership officers.
- [x] **Bank Account Validation**: Supported multi-currency settlement corporate banking credentials.

## 3. AI & Generative Chatbot Governance Standards
Deploying customer-facing artificial intelligence requires compliance with evolving global AI statutory frameworks and deceptive trade practice rules.

### AI Governance & Transparency Checklist
- [x] **EU AI Act Article 50 & FTC Transparency Disclosure**:
  * Under the European Union Artificial Intelligence Act and FTC guidelines, individuals interacting with automated conversational systems must be transparently informed that they are communicating with an artificial AI entity rather than a live human agent.
  * **Implementation Need**: Ensure the chat widget interface (`widget.js` and Studio presets) permanently displays an attribution badge (e.g., *"Powered by Zeva AI"* or *"Automated AI Assistant"*) prior to human live-agent handoffs.
- [x] **Enterprise AI Data Sovereignty Guarantee**:
  * Commercial bot owners uploading internal business manuals, pricing inventories, and operational workflows require explicit legal assurances within the Terms of Service that their proprietary documents and chat transcripts **will never be utilized to train public foundational AI models** (e.g., OpenAI GPT or Meta Llama weights).
- [x] **High-Risk Medical & Legal Exemption**:
  * Clear disclaimers prohibiting the utilization of Zeva AI for unsupervised automated decision-making in high-risk sectors (emergency medical diagnosis, formal legal counseling, automated employment screening, or credit underwriting).

## 4. B2B SaaS Contractual Agreements & Security Certifications
Securing Pro and Enterprise business subscribers requires standardized Business-to-Business (B2B) contractual frameworks and demonstrated information security postures.

### B2B Legal & Security Checklist
- [x] **Data Processing Addendum (DPA)**:
  * A mandatory legal agreement established between Zeva (as Data Processor) and commercial bot owners (as Data Controllers) governing cross-border transfers of visitor lead info using established Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).
- [x] **Subprocessor Registry Transparency**:
  * Maintain an accessible, updated inventory of authorized third-party infrastructure entities processing platform payloads:
    1. **Neon Inc.** (PostgreSQL database server hosting).
    2. **OpenRouter / OpenAI** (LLM generative chat inference engines).
    3. **Stripe Inc. & Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd.** (Payment subscription processing).
    4. **Vercel / Render / Railway / Cloudflare** (Application edge runtime & static content hosting).
- [x] **Security Attestations Roadmap (SOC 2 Type II / ISO 27001)**:
  * Prepare institutional documentation illustrating encryption at rest (AES-256 / Fernet database field encryption), TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, and stringent multi-tenant isolation via PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) to satisfy Enterprise cybersecurity questionnaires and pave the way for formal SOC 2 Type II certification audits.
