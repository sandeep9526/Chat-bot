Ochreshift AI Overview & Features:
Ochreshift AI is an advanced RAG-powered AI chat widget designed for businesses, salons, clinics, real estate agencies, e-commerce stores, and service providers. It reads your website, FAQs, and documents (PDFs, TXT, Docs) and answers customer questions 24/7 with cited sources — never making up facts.

Key Features of Ochreshift AI:
1. 100% Grounded Answers: Every answer is backed by your own uploaded documents or website content with source citations.
2. Real-time Lead Capture: Automatically captures hot and warm leads, scoring buying intent and storing contact details.
3. Multi-Channel Integration: Supports Meta WhatsApp Cloud API integration, Outbound Webhooks, Email alerts, and real-time Google Sheets sync.
4. Studio Customizer: Full whitelabel customization allowing you to pick brand accent colors, custom logo, fonts, launcher style, and industry presets (Salon, Clinic, Real Estate, E-Commerce, Coaching).
5. 1-Line Script Embed: Simple script tag installation on any website platform including WordPress, Shopify, Next.js, HTML, and Wix.

How Ochreshift Works:
1. Feed Content: Enter your website URL or upload your PDFs/FAQs.
2. Customize Look: Adjust widget appearance in the Studio.
3. Embed & Sync: Copy your 1-line embed snippet and connect real-time Google Sheets or email notifications.
